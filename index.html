Questions

What is GameBeeper?

A free, open-source video game news aggregator. It gathers the latest news, reviews, reveals and trailers from 27 trusted gaming sources — including PlayStation Blog, Xbox Wire, Nintendo Life, IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer, Polygon and PC Gamer — into one fast, ad-free feed.

How often is the feed updated?

Every hour, from all 34 source feeds. Press Refresh (or R) to load the newest build, or turn on auto-refresh in Settings.

Which platforms and topics does it cover?

PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, PC gaming, indie games, reviews, trailers and reveals, esports, gaming hardware and the games industry. Pick a topic above the feed to focus on one.

Can I tailor the feed?

Yes. Use Customize to hide topics, mute sources, limit stories by age or hide sponsored posts. Tap the bookmark on any story to save it for later — saved stories and settings stay in your browser.

Does GameBeeper show ads or track me?

No ads, ever. Google Analytics only runs if you accept it, and you can switch it off any time in Settings. See the privacy policy for details.
